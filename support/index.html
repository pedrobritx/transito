Is Transito free?

The repository is public and the app has no subscription mechanism. Available builds and their licence terms are documented on GitHub.

Can it download any video?

No. Transito targets compatible HLS/M3U8 streams. DRM-protected, encrypted or inaccessible streams may not work.

What is M3U8?

An M3U8 file is a playlist used by HLS streaming systems to reference media segments or stream variants.

Why is FFmpeg required?

FFmpeg handles media processing. The current app can offer to download the required binary when it is missing.

Why can't it find a link on a website?

Discovery searches exposed playlist references in the page; authentication, dynamic playback, encryption and DRM can prevent discovery.

Why is macOS warning me?

CI builds may be unsigned. Verify the download origin, review the system warning and use the per-app option in Privacy & Security if appropriate. Never disable Gatekeeper globally.

Where does the file go?

You choose the output path. The app initially suggests video.mp4 inside Downloads, and offers Show in Finder after success.

Is it in the Mac App Store?

No. Transito is distributed through GitHub.
